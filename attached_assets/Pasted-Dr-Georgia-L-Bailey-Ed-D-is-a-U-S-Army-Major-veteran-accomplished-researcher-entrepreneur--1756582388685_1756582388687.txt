Dr. Georgia L. Bailey, Ed.D. is a U.S. Army Major (veteran), accomplished researcher,
entrepreneur, and senior executive with more than three decades of leadership spanning military
service, federal research and development, business consulting, and corporate training.
Dr. Bailey began her career as a U.S. Army Chemical and Systems Automation Officer, where
she served as an instructor and master course developer at the U.S. Army Transportation School,
Fort Eustis, VA, creating a 40-hour curriculum in Nuclear, Biological, Radiological, and
Chemical (NBRC) operations that was delivered to more than 1,500 U.S. and allied officers. Her
instructional excellence carried into her overseas service at the 97th General Hospital in
Frankfurt, Germany, where she directed NBC mass casualty U.S.–German emergency response
training and readiness exercises.
In her subsequent assignment at the National Security Agency (NSA), Dr. Bailey played a
pivotal role in advanced security research, leading the Logical Co-processing Kernel (LOCK)
initiative to achieve A-1 level security at the operating system level. She also co-led a joint
NSA/National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) project to formally describe a
network-layer security protocol for the OSI model, providing groundbreaking advances in
protecting wired and wireless communications. For her contributions, she was awarded two Joint
Meritorious Service Medals.
After transitioning from active duty, Dr. Bailey pursued a career blending education, leadership,
and applied technology. She earned her Doctor of Education in Organizational Leadership from
Argosy University, a Master of Science in Information Technology from Johns Hopkins
University, and completed additional advanced studies in systems engineering and project
management.
In 2012, she founded Asgard Group, LLC, a consulting and solutions firm dedicated to providing
physical, logical, and technical security services and training. Under her leadership, Asgard
secured contract awards supporting clients such as the FBI, U.S. Army Intelligence Command,
and the Pentagon Force Protection Agency. Dr. Bailey has guided organizations to secure over
$800 million in contract awards through business development, capture management, proposal
leadership, and program execution, while also mentoring small and mid-sized businesses in
government contracting.
A recognized thought leader, Dr. Bailey has delivered keynote lectures and training on topics
including information security, emergency response planning, proposal development, and
business growth strategies. Her career reflects a consistent commitment to advancing national
security, developing innovative solutions, and empowering organizations to thrive through
visionary leadership and disciplined execution.
